Technical Model Architecture Overview

ResNet50-CBAM Spatial Branch

Extracts 2,048-dimensional high-level topological feature maps with Channel & Spatial Attention gating for localized scratches.

EfficientNet-B0 Texture Branch

Extracts 1,280-dimensional fine-grained surface die textures with MBConv inverted residual blocks.

Cross-Attention Fusion Layer

Fuses spatial and textural representation vectors into a dynamic 3,328-dimensional joint vector.

Focal Loss & SWA Training

Combats heavy class imbalance ($\gamma=2.0$) and stabilizes weight convergence across 50 epochs.
